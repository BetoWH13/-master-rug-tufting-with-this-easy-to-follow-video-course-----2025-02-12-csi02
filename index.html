About Us

Founded in 2020 by a collective of passionate rug artists and educators, Tufting.xyz emerged from a simple vision: to make professional rug tufting accessible to everyone. Our journey began in a small studio where we taught local workshops, and has grown into a global community of creators.

Our mission is to empower creative individuals with the skills, knowledge, and confidence to create exceptional custom rugs. We believe in combining traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques, fostering a supportive community, and promoting sustainable practices in the rug-making industry.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any prior experience?

Absolutely not! Our course is designed for complete beginners. We start with the basics and gradually progress to advanced techniques, ensuring you build a strong foundation.

What equipment do I need?

You'll need a tufting gun, frame, primary backing fabric, yarn, and basic tools. We provide a detailed equipment guide with recommended suppliers and budget-friendly options.

How long does it take to complete the course?

The course includes 40+ hours of content, but you can progress at your own pace. Most students complete the basics within 2-3 weeks and achieve advanced proficiency within 2-3 months.

Is there ongoing support?

Yes! You'll get access to our private community forum where you can ask questions, share progress, and connect with other students. Our instructors regularly provide feedback and support.

What's your refund policy?

We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with the course, simply contact our support team for a full refund, no questions asked.
